# Privacy Policy

**FlowTime** — Last updated: March 25, 2026

## Overview

FlowTime is a macOS menu bar time tracking application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data FlowTime collects, how it is used, and your rights.

## Data Collection

FlowTime does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data is stored locally on your Mac.

### What FlowTime stores locally:
- Projects, tasks, and time entries you create
- App preferences and settings
- Database backups (stored on your Mac or in your personal iCloud Drive)

### What FlowTime does NOT do:
- Send data to external servers
- Use analytics or tracking services
- Collect crash reports
- Access your contacts, location, or browsing history
- Display advertisements

## iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your time tracking data is stored in your personal iCloud Drive container. This data is governed by [Apple's iCloud privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). FlowTime does not have access to your iCloud credentials or any other data in your iCloud account.

## System Permissions

FlowTime may request the following macOS permissions:

- **Input Monitoring** (optional): Used solely to detect idle time so the app can ask what to do with untracked time when you return. This data is never stored or transmitted.
- **Notifications** (optional): Used to send local reminders about idle time and daily goals.

## Data Retention

All data remains on your device (or in your personal iCloud Drive) until you choose to delete it. You can delete all data at any time from Settings > Data > Danger Zone > Delete All Data.

## Children's Privacy

FlowTime does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted here with a revised date.
